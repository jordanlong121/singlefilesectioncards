## Cell biology

### What does the mitochondrion do?
Hint: think energy. #biology

%% flip %%
It makes **ATP** by aerobic respiration — the cell's power supply. Has its own DNA, inherited from the mother.

### Prokaryote vs. eukaryote
Which one has a nucleus?

%% flip %%
**Eukaryotes** have a membrane-bound nucleus and organelles. **Prokaryotes** (bacteria, archaea) keep their DNA loose in the cytoplasm.

### Where does the Krebs cycle happen?

%% flip %%
In the **mitochondrial matrix**. Acetyl-CoA in, NADH, FADH₂ and CO₂ out — two turns per glucose.

### Osmosis
Water moves from ___ to ___ concentration?

%% flip %%
From **low solute** to **high solute** concentration, across a semi-permeable membrane, until balanced.

### Photosynthesis, in one line
Reactants on the left, products on the right.
- [ ] review again Friday

%% flip %%
**6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂**. Happens in the chloroplasts; the oxygen comes from the water.

## Physics

### Ohm's law
State it, and name the units. #physics

%% flip %%
**V = I × R** — volts, amperes, ohms. Double the resistance at the same voltage and the current halves.

### Newton's second law
The one with the equation.

%% flip %%
**F = m × a**. Force in newtons: 1 N accelerates 1 kg at 1 m/s².

### Speed of light
To three significant figures, in a vacuum.

%% flip %%
**3.00 × 10⁸ m/s** — about 300,000 km every second. Nothing with mass reaches it.

### Wavelength and frequency
How are they related?

%% flip %%
Inversely: **c = f × λ**. Longer waves, lower frequency — radio at one end, gamma rays at the other.

### Kinetic energy
The formula, and what doubling the speed does.

%% flip %%
**KE = ½ m v²**. Double the speed and the energy goes up **four** times — which is why braking distance balloons.

## Spanish vocabulary

### el gato
Noun. Also the name of the shop cat. #spanish

%% flip %%
**the cat**. *El gato duerme en la ventana* — the cat sleeps in the window.

### la biblioteca
Noun. Where the flash cards were written.

%% flip %%
**the library**. Careful — not "bookshop", that's *la librería*.

### Ser vs. estar
Both mean "to be". When is which?

%% flip %%
**Ser** for what something *is* (identity, origin, time). **Estar** for how or where it is right now (state, location).

### ¿Dónde está el baño?
The most useful sentence in the deck.

%% flip %%
**Where is the bathroom?** *Está al fondo, a la derecha* — at the back, on the right.

### tener que + infinitive
A very common construction. What does it express?

%% flip %%
**Having to** do something. *Tengo que estudiar* — I have to study.

### Preterite vs. imperfect
Two past tenses. The rule of thumb?
- [ ] drill with the workbook, ch. 7

%% flip %%
**Preterite** for completed, one-off events; **imperfect** for ongoing, habitual, or background action. *Llovía cuando llegué* — it was raining when I arrived.
